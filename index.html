Research and analysis

Quantitative models and strategy work from my internship, live projects and MBA coursework.

  • Bankruptcy prediction from annual-report text

    Summer internship, Quantropy Research and Solution LLP, 2026

    Built a pipeline that scrapes annual reports of about 167 listed and delisted Indian companies from BSE and NSE, extracts the Management Discussion and Analysis section, and trains embedding, CNN and BiLSTM classifiers to flag financial distress. The distress label came from compulsory delistings (67 distressed against 100 healthy firms). Much of the work went into keeping the evaluation honest: company-level splits to prevent leakage, focal loss for class imbalance, and calibrated decision thresholds. The result is an inference engine that scores any new annual report's distress probability in seconds.

    • Python
    • PyMuPDF
    • Deep learning
    • NLP

    Benchmarked JPMorgan's investment banking franchise (M&A, ECM and DCM) against Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley. Built a five-year Excel model of revenue, profit and fee mix, and gave a SWOT-based Buy, Hold or Sell call.
